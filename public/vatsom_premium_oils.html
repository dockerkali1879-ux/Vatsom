THE PARFUMEUR’S SCROLL

Each composition is inscribed on laid cotton paper and undergoes three unhurried acts of classical British perfumery.

01
Sartorial Accord Selection

First-Cut Absolutes & Rare Resins

Three-year aged Florentine iris butter, Scottish highland peat, Calabrian bergamot, and wild Assam agarwood are weighed by hand in micro-lots.

Purity: First-Cut Absolute
Cellar: 15°C Constant
02
Charred Oak & Amphora Ageing

120-Day Extrait Maceration

Rested at 26% to 38% concentration in seasoned oak and dark glass carboys until top, heart, and base notes fuse into an unbroken velvet chord.

Oil Concentration: 26% – 38%
Maceration: 2,880 Hours
03
Chamois Filtration & Wax Seal

Heavy Cut-Crystal & Hand-Numbered Dossier

Chilled and filtered through natural chamois cloth to preserve natural oils, decanted into obsidian crystal flacons, and sealed in crimson wax.

Sillage: 14+ Hours
